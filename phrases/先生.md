<span style="font-size: 6em;">{先生|せん|せい}</span>
a way to address someone of a superior position or renowned standing in an academic or artistic field. people to be addressed as "sensei" include but are not limited to: school teacher, teacher in any other capacity, doctor, scientist, artist,  literary author, artisans such as blacksmiths, carpenters, potters, jewelers and culinary masters, martial artists that are ranked at the top of their field or take on disciples.
