<span style="font-size: 6em;">{姉|あね}</span>
big sister, mostly used in absence of the person in question.

can be used with another word to mean a fellow member of a close-knit group with a pseudo familial bond, such as an older / more tenured fellow live-in student of an artisan instructing a small group of people, or older / more tenured members of a mob / yakuza under the same boss.
