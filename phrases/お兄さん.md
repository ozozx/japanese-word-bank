<span style="font-size: 6em;">{お兄さん||にい||}</span>
big brother, used also as a second person address to an older brother (or a person who fulfills a similar function without biological connection)

can be used to address a stranger that's older, but not by a lot. (or to compliment far older people by insinuating a smaller age gap.)
